Swasthya is a coaching tool, not a doctor. It does not diagnose conditions,
and it never changes, starts or stops medication. It will tell you when
something should go to a doctor, and it will stop coaching and point you to
help if you describe an emergency. Your health data stays in your account and
is never used for advertising or shared with third parties.
